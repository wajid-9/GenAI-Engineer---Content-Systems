1. RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
2. A language model only knows what it saw during training. It does not know your private or new documents.
3. RAG lets the model look up relevant documents first, then answer using them.
4. The steps are: split documents into chunks, turn chunks into embeddings (lists of numbers), store them in a vector database.
5. At question time, the question is also turned into an embedding, and the closest chunks are retrieved.
6. The retrieved chunks are added to the prompt, and the model writes the answer from them.
7. RAG does NOT retrain or change the model. It only changes the prompt.
8. RAG reduces made-up answers (hallucinations) and lets answers cite their sources, but it does not remove them fully.
